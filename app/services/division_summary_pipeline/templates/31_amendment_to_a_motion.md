**Jargon Explainer:** *An amendment proposes changing the words of a motion before the chamber decides it, by leaving words out, putting new words in, or both. The chamber votes on the amendment first. If it is agreed to, the motion is later put to the chamber in its changed form; if not, the motion is put as it was. This vote decides only the change, not the motion itself.*
